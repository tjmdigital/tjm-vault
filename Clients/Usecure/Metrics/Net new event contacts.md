# Net new event contacts

**890** across all events. Someone with no CRM record before the event brought them in.

Covers **registrants as well as attendees** - a new person who registered and did not show
is still a lead the event generated. Confirmed by Hetty, 17 August.

Webinars use the **exact registration timestamp** from the marketing event, so a contact
counts if their record was created at that moment. That took webinars from 55 to 186 - the
old "created on or after the event date" rule missed almost everyone who registered early.
