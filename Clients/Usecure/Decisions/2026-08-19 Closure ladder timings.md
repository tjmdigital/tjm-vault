# Closure ladder timings: 2 / 3 / 14

> "I think the warning needs to come 2 days after the lead is under ownership, after 3 days
> if no follow up, we should move to an SDR to work. After 14 days we can then close."

## How this came about

Worth keeping, because it shows how a decision drifts. On 11 August Molly said *"if they're
not going to follow up after a week, then they deserve to have that email"* and separately
Tom proposed a *"closed in 3 days unless you take action"* countdown. Built as 7 / 3 / 14.

Molly then read it back on 19 August and said she had understood 3 days to reassignment
from arrival. Neither party was wrong - two numbers from one conversation had been
assembled into a ladder nobody had actually specified.

Resolved by quoting both original lines back to her and letting her state the numbers
directly. **The fix was quoting, not explaining.**
