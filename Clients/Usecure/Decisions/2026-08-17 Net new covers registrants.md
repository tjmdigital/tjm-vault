# Net new covers registrants, and webinars use registration time

Two questions put to Hetty, both answered same day.

> "1. Yes - please tag ALL new contacts (regardless as attended or no-shows) as net new
> 2. Yes - makes total sense happy with that approach!"

So net new covers everyone the event touched, not just attendees - a new person who
registered and did not show is still a lead the event generated.

And webinars count a contact as net new if their record was created at the exact moment
they registered, taken from the marketing event, rather than "created on or after the
event date". In-person events keep the old rule because there is no registration record.

Result: [[Net new event contacts]] went to 890, webinars from 55 to 186.
