# A note counts as activity, and the warning prompts disqualification

Two refinements raised on the 18 August call.

**A note counts as working it.** Where a rep deliberately leaves a lead alone - an MSP
client who should not be approached directly, for example - adding a note pauses the ladder.
Nobody gets chased for a decision they have already made and recorded.

**The reminder prompts disqualification.** No lead had ever been disqualified with a reason.
The warning email now asks the owner to close it properly with a reason rather than let it
time out, because that feedback is worth more than an automatic close.

Both in [[Stale lead ladder (Usecure)]].
